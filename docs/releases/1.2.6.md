# VirtualDeck v1.2.6

## Highlights

- **Hydration tracker:** A dedicated **Tools → Hydration** panel with a live preview of your stream overlay while you tune settings.
- **Five gauge styles:** Pick a look that fits your stream, including a clean **minimal** gauge with animated water fill.
- **Make it yours:** Custom water and accent colors, plus **sparkle** and **confetti** effects that render inside the water, with a smooth **moving wave** as you fill up.
- **Sounds:** Choose a sound that plays when someone logs hydration (and an optional goal sound).
- **Channel points:** Tie logging to a specific reward with the **channel point reward** dropdown, or fall back to matching rewards whose title includes your keyword.
- **Quick controls:** Log water from a **deck button**, a **hotkey**, or Twitch redemptions; manual **reset** and go-live/midnight reset behavior are more reliable.
- **Overlay connection:** The browser source overlay stays in sync over WebSocket—updates, sounds, and effects fire on the overlay reliably.

## Upgrade notes

- In-app auto-update from **1.2.5** uses the same GitHub Release artifacts (`latest.yml`, installer, blockmap).
- Twitch OAuth already includes the scopes needed for custom rewards (`channel:read:redemptions` and `channel:manage:redemptions`). Reconnect Twitch in **Settings** if redemptions do not show up.
